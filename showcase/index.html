AI Poker Bot stands on eighty years of game theory. In 1944 John von Neumann used a stripped-down poker to show why a good player has to bluff. In 1950 Harold Kuhn solved a three-card version by hand. In 2007 a team at the University of Alberta introduced counterfactual regret minimisation, and in 2017 Libratus used it to beat four professionals at heads-up no-limit. Pluribus followed in 2019, at six-player tables. This project rebuilds that line from scratch, in Python on one laptop: its own engine, its own network, sixty rounds of self-play. It is far weaker than its ancestors, and it still learned the same lesson: with nothing on the river, bet some of the time. Sit down and play it.
